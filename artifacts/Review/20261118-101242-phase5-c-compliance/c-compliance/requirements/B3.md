Trace reasoning: at front-obstacle Tick t, movement becomes Stopping and the step timer starts at zero. Ticks t+1..t+4 keep Stop. At t+5, done selects the open side and resets elapsed to zero; the turn lasts through t+9. At t+10 the state becomes Forward and cleaning starts/powers up in that same cycle. Both turn branches share this completion logic. Backing and repeated backing remain Avoiding, so cannot leak a cleaning-on command. On a turn-ending Tick with front still blocked, code returns Forward once and tests the obstacle on the next Tick, exactly as approved T12/T14 specify; this is not an invented physical collision guarantee.
